# CV Search

A system that lets an HR team ingest CVs and find the candidates who best match a need expressed in natural language.

## Language

### Documents and people

**CV**:
A document file describing one person's career, as submitted to the system.
_Avoid_: Résumé, document, file

**Profile**:
The structured representation extracted from exactly one CV.
_Avoid_: Record, parsed CV, candidate

**Candidate**:
The person a CV describes. Two CVs for the same candidate yield two profiles.
_Avoid_: Applicant, consultant, person

### Career

**Position**:
A role a candidate held at an organisation over a period of time.
_Avoid_: Job, experience

**Internship**:
A position held as a trainee. It does not count towards years of experience.
_Avoid_: Stage, traineeship

**Sector**:
The line of business a position belongs to, such as consulting or retail.
_Avoid_: Industry, domain

**Years of experience**:
Total time spent in positions other than internships, with overlapping periods counted once.
_Avoid_: Tenure, career length

**Stated experience**:
The number of years a CV declares for itself, used when its positions carry no dates.

**Sector experience**:
Years of experience restricted to positions in one sector.

**Seniority**:
A band derived from years of experience: junior (under 2), confirmed (2 to 5), senior (5 or more).
_Avoid_: Level, grade
